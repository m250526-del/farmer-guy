# Cotton (کپاس / Phutti)

## Overview
Cotton is the lifeline of Pakistan's textile industry, primarily cultivated in southern Punjab (Multan, Bahawalpur, Khanewal, Rahim Yar Khan) and Sindh (Sukkur, Ghotki, Sanghar). Sowing typically commences from mid-April to late May. Recommended BT varieties include IUB-13, BS-15, FH-333, and MNH-886.

## Sowing & Seed Rate
- Seed rate for delinted seed: 6 to 8 kg per acre.
- Row-to-row spacing: 2.5 feet (75 cm); plant-to-plant spacing: 9 to 12 inches.
- Seed treatment: Imidacloprid or Thiamethoxam @ 4-5g per kg of seed protects seedlings against sucking pests for the initial 30 days.

## Irrigation Schedule
Cotton requires 5 to 7 irrigations depending on monsoon rainfall and soil type.
- First irrigation: 30 to 35 days after sowing for flat planting; 3 to 5 days after sowing for bed-furrow sowing.
- Subsequent irrigations: 12 to 15 days interval during peak summer; reduce interval to 7 to 10 days during flowering and boll formation.
- Critical stages: Peak flowering and boll enlargement. Moisture stress at these stages causes heavy square and boll shedding. Stop watering when 60% of bolls have opened.

## Fertilizer Management
For medium fertile soils per acre:
- Nitrogen (N): 2 to 3 bags of Urea. Apply 1/3 at thinning, 1/3 at first flower, and 1/3 at peak flowering.
- Phosphorus (P): 1 to 1.5 bags of DAP applied entirely at sowing.
- Potassium (K): 1 bag of SOP or MOP at squaring stage.
- Micronutrients: Foliar spray of Zinc Sulphate (0.5%) and Boric acid (0.1%) at flowering enhances boll retention.

## Pests & Management
### Whitefly (سفید مکھی)
- Economic Threshold Level (ETL): 5 adults or nymphs per leaf.
- Symptoms: Nymphs suck sap from underside of leaves; secrete honeydew leading to sooty mold (black leaves). Vector for Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV).
- Management: Spray Pyriproxyfen @ 500ml/acre for nymphs, or Diafenthiuron @ 200g/acre or Flonicamid @ 60-80g/acre for adults. Avoid synthetic pyrethroids which flare up whitefly.

### Pink Bollworm (گلابی سنڈی)
- ETL: 5% rosetted flowers or 5% live larvae in green bolls.
- Symptoms: Larvae bore into flowers and green bolls, feeding on developing seeds and staining lint.
- Management: Install pheromone traps @ 5 to 8 traps/acre for monitoring. Apply Spinetoram @ 100ml/acre or Chlorantraniliprole @ 50ml/acre at early boll formation.

### Jassid / Green Leafhopper (تیلا)
- ETL: 1 adult or nymph per leaf.
- Symptoms: Hopper burn; leaf margins turn yellow, curl downwards, and turn reddish-brown.
- Management: Flonicamid @ 60g/acre or Dinotefuran @ 100g/acre.
