# Mango (آم)

## Overview
Mango is the 'King of Fruits' and Pakistan's second largest fruit crop, centered in southern Punjab (Multan, Khanewal, Muzaffargarh, Rahim Yar Khan) and Sindh (Mirpur Khas, Hyderabad, Tando Allahyar). Celebrated cultivars include Sindhri, Chaunsa (Samar Bahisht), Anwar Ratol, Dussehri, and Langra.

## Canopy Management & Pruning
- Pruning window: Immediately after harvest (July-August). Remove dead, diseased, criss-cross branches and water sprouts to allow sunlight penetration into the inner canopy.
- After pruning, apply Copper Oxychloride paste on cut surfaces to prevent stem rot and Dieback.

## Irrigation Schedule
- Non-flowering months: Irrigate at 15 to 20 days interval during summer, 25 to 30 days during winter.
- Flower bud differentiation (November to January): Withhold irrigation (stress period) to stimulate floral initiation instead of vegetative flush.
- Fruit setting stage (March to June): Resume regular irrigations at 7 to 10 days interval. Severe drought during fruit development triggers excessive fruit drop (chana drop).
- Withhold irrigation 15 days before harvest to prevent fruit softness and anthracnose development.

## Fertilizer Schedule
For a mature bearing tree (10+ years):
- Farmyard Manure (FYM): 40 to 50 kg per tree applied in December-January.
- Nitrogen (Urea): 1.5 to 2 kg per tree. Apply half at fruit set (March) and half after harvest (July-August).
- Phosphorus (DAP): 1.5 kg per tree applied in January.
- Potassium (SOP): 1.5 kg per tree applied at marble-size fruit stage (April) to optimize fruit sizing, sugar brix, and shelf life.
- Micronutrients: Foliar spray of Zinc Sulphate (0.2%) and Borax (0.1%) during early fruit development.

## Pest & Disease Management
### Mango Anthracnose (کالا دھبہ)
- Symptoms: Dark brown to black spots on leaves, blossoms, and ripening fruits. Flowers blight and drop.
- Management: Spray Difenoconazole or Azoxystrobin @ 0.5ml per liter water before flowering and after fruit set.

### Mango Powdery Mildew (سفید پھپھوندی)
- Symptoms: White powdery fungal growth on panicles, flowers, and fruitlets during cloudy, cool spring days.
- Management: Spray Wettable Sulphur @ 2g/L or Penconazole @ 0.5ml/L at panicle emergence before flowers open.

### Mango Mealybug (مالٹا / آم کا بگ)
- Symptoms: Pinkish nymphs crawl up tree trunks in January-February, clustering on shoots and sucking sap.
- Management: Wrap 6-inch slippery plastic bands around tree trunks in December. Apply Chlorpyrifos around tree basins.
