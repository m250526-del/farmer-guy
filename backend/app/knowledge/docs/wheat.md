# Wheat (گندم)

## Overview
Wheat is Pakistan's premier food grain crop, cultivated across Punjab, Sindh, and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Rabi season planting begins in early November and harvest occurs from April to May. Top recommended varieties include Akbar-2019, Dilkash-2020, Subhani-2021, and Fakhar-e-Bhakkar.

## Sowing & Seed Rate
- Optimum sowing window: November 1 to November 20. Sowing delayed past November 25 incurs an estimated 1% yield loss per day due to terminal heat stress.
- Seed rate: 50 kg per acre for timely sowing; increase to 60 kg per acre after November 25.
- Sowing method: Drill sowing with 9-inch row spacing is strongly preferred over broadcasting (Chhatta).

## Irrigation Schedule
Wheat requires 4 to 5 irrigations in canal-irrigated zones:
- 1st irrigation (Crown Root Initiation - CRI): 20 to 25 days after sowing. Most critical irrigation; missing it causes stunted tillering.
- 2nd irrigation (Tillering to Jointing): 45 to 50 days after sowing.
- 3rd irrigation (Booting to Heading): 70 to 80 days after sowing.
- 4th irrigation (Milking / Grain Formation): 90 to 100 days after sowing. Avoid irrigation during strong winds to prevent crop lodging (گرنا).
- 5th irrigation (Dough stage): 110 to 120 days after sowing (if needed on light soils).

## Fertilizer Management
Recommended doses per acre for irrigated fertile soils:
- DAP: 1.5 to 2 bags applied at soil preparation / sowing.
- Urea: 2 to 2.5 bags. Apply 1 bag at first irrigation (CRI stage) and 1 bag at second irrigation.
- Potash (SOP): 1 bag at sowing to increase grain weight and heat tolerance.
- Zinc Sulphate (33%): 6 kg per acre applied with first irrigation.

## Weed Control
- Broadleaf weeds (چوڑے پتے والی جڑی بوٹیاں - Bathu, Lehli, Senji): Spray Bromoxynil + MCPA @ 400-500ml/acre 30-35 days after sowing.
- Narrow-leaf weeds (نوکیلے پتے والی جڑی بوٹیاں - Dumbi sitti, Jangli Jai): Spray Clodinafop-propargyl @ 120g/acre or Pinoxaden (Axial) @ 330ml/acre after 1st irrigation at moist soil conditions (watter).

## Diseases & Management
### Rusts (کنگی - Yellow / Stripe Rust & Brown / Leaf Rust)
- Symptoms: Yellow rust causes bright yellow pustules arranged in linear stripes on leaves during cool, damp weather (January-February). Leaf rust forms round orange-brown pustules.
- Management: Spray Tebuconazole or Azoxystrobin + Difenoconazole @ 200ml/acre upon initial symptom detection.
