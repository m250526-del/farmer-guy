# Rice (چاول / دھان)

## Overview
Rice is a major export crop and dietary staple, grown extensively in Punjab's Kalar tract (Sheikhupura, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Hafizabad) for aromatic Basmati varieties, and in Sindh (Larkana, Jacobabad, Badin) for coarse IRRI varieties.

## Varieties & Sowing Time
- Basmati types: Super Basmati, Basmati 515, PK-1121 Aromatic, Chenab Basmati. Nursery sowing: May 20 to June 20.
- Coarse / Non-Basmati types: KSK-133, IRRI-6, Fakhr-e-Makan. Nursery sowing: May 1 to May 25.
- Transplantation: 25 to 30 days old seedlings transplanted into puddled fields.

## Plant Population & Spacing
- Target 80,000 to 100,000 plants per acre (minimum 2 seedlings per hill with 9x9 inch spacing).

## Irrigation & Water Management
- Keep standing water of 1.5 to 2 inches for the first 30 days after transplanting to suppress weed growth and establish roots.
- Intermittent drying (Alternate Wetting and Drying - AWD) after 30 days saves up to 30% water without reducing yield.
- Drain field completely 10 to 15 days before harvest to facilitate uniform grain drying and mechanical combining.

## Fertilizer Recommendations
Per acre:
- DAP: 1.5 bags at land puddling before transplanting.
- Urea: 2 bags applied in splits: 1 bag at 30 days, 1 bag at 45 to 50 days (panicle initiation). Avoid late urea application which promotes Bakanae disease and lodging.
- Potash: 1 bag SOP at puddling improves grain aroma, milling recovery, and stem strength.
- Zinc Sulphate (33%): 5 to 6 kg per acre at 10 to 15 days after transplanting to prevent Khaira disease (zinc deficiency).

## Insect Pests & Diseases
### Rice Stem Borers (تنے کی سنڈی - Yellow & White Borers)
- Symptoms: Dead hearts in vegetative stage (drying of central shoot); White heads in reproductive stage (empty white panicles).
- Control: Broadcast Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 9 kg/acre or Fipronil 0.3G @ 8 kg/acre in standing water 25 to 30 days after transplanting.

### Bacterial Leaf Blight - BLB (پتوں کا جھلساؤ)
- Symptoms: Water-soaked lesions starting at leaf margins, turning yellow to grayish-white wavy stripes.
- Management: Avoid stagnant water; stop nitrogen application; spray Copper Oxychloride @ 500g/acre or Kasugamycin.
