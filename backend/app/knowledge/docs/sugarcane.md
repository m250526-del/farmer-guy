# Sugarcane (کماد / گنا)

## Overview
Sugarcane is Pakistan's primary cash crop for sugar production, cultivated in Punjab (Faisalabad, Sargodha, Rahim Yar Khan), Sindh, and parts of KP. Autumn planting (September-October) provides 20-25% higher cane yield and sugar recovery than spring planting (February-March). Recommended commercial varieties include CPF-249, CPF-253, HSF-240, and Thatta-10.

## Seed Rate & Planting
- Seed rate: 70 to 80 maunds (2,800 to 3,200 kg) per acre, or 35,000 to 40,000 double-budded setts.
- Trench planting at 4-foot row spacing facilitates mechanical interculture and lodging prevention.
- Seed sett treatment: Dip setts in a solution of Topsin-M (Thiophanate-Methyl) @ 2g per liter water before planting to eliminate red rot fungal spores.

## Irrigation Schedule
Sugarcane is a high-water-demand perennial crop requiring 16 to 20 irrigations:
- Germination & Tillering (March to May): 10 to 12 days interval.
- Grand Growth stage (June to August): 7 to 10 days interval. Water stress during hot summer months stunts internode elongation.
- Ripening stage (September to November): 20 to 25 days interval. Stop watering 25 days before harvest to concentrate sucrose.

## Fertilizer Requirements
Per acre for plant crop:
- DAP: 2 to 2.5 bags at planting in trenches.
- Urea: 3.5 to 4 bags. Apply in 3 equal splits: after complete germination, in April, and finish by mid-June before earthing-up.
- SOP (Potassium): 1 to 2 bags applied at sowing or first earthing up to enhance frost and drought resistance.

## Major Pests & Control
### Sugarcane Gurdaspur Borer & Top Borer
- Symptoms: Top borer causes 'bunchy top' and dead heart with a shot-hole pattern on leaves. Gurdaspur borer causes withered tops easily broken by wind.
- Management: Apply Carbofuran 3G @ 12-14 kg/acre or Chlorantraniliprole (Ferterra) @ 4 kg/acre in soil during June-July.

### Whitefly & Pyrilla (کماد کا پتیلا)
- Symptoms: Leaves covered with dark sooty mold; stunted cane growth and reduced sucrose percentage.
- Management: Conserve natural parasitoids (*Epiricania melanoleuca*). If population exceeds threshold, spray Chlorpyrifos @ 1 liter/acre.
